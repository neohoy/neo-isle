# Surface: gallery (docs/index.html)

Mode: Experience. Visitors come to look at isles; the interface recedes.

Audience and job: see a running isle within seconds, try other looks on it, browse the isles, then find the brief template and the install command.

Pinned by the owner: follow NEO-Cine's gallery idea (a place to preview), bilingual, name NEO-Isle · 方寸之境.

## Direction contract

THESIS: the first viewport is a live isle you can turn, not a picture of one. The category default (a wordmark over a looping video) is declined.
WORLD: inherited from the isles' own interface: near-black space ground, hairline borders, light type with wide tracking, one warm lamp accent for active state. NEO-Cine's hard-shadow poster look is its own and is not copied; only its page order is.
FIRST VIEWPORT: full-bleed running isle; wordmark and one line bottom-left; a dock bottom-centre that changes isle, container, backdrop, glow and hour on the running isle.
VISITOR PATH: live isle and dock, then isles as wide plates with a day-to-night strip, then the look table, then the brief template with a copy action, then install, then close.
SIGNATURE INTERACTION: choosing a container or backdrop rebuilds the same isle in that look in front of the visitor.
RISK: a WebGL isle is heavy on phones; a still stands in until the isle reports ready, and stays if it never does.

seed: none (direction pinned by the owner's brief; no roll)
